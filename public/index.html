How CivicLens Works

From citizen camera to district ground-team resolution in 3 seamless steps.

Step 1

Snap Photo & Auto-GPS

Upload the civic issue photo. The system accesses device hardware GPS and reverse-geocodes the exact postal PIN code and landmark.

Step 2

Pincode-Based Officer Dispatch

The complaint is mapped to the designated District Sub-Admin responsible for that specific PIN code and municipal department.

Step 3

Verified Resolution Proof

The officer updates status, dispatches field contractors, and uploads verified "After" photos to Cloudinary to close the grievance.
